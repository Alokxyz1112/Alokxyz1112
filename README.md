Hey, I'm Alok. I'm a biotech student who likes building things. Most of my time goes into learning bioinformatics and building my own startup. On the side I make Android apps, and lately I've been sending small fixes to the Flutter and Dart docs.

Okay, real talk. My own study days kept slipping into the scroll. One Short, then another, and the evening's gone. So I'm building the app I wanted for myself.

It's called [Vedrino](https://vedrino.website). Plan less. Flow more. It's Android only, still in progress, and the beta is in December.

<details>
<summary><b>What already works in Vedrino</b></summary>
<br/>

The part I care about most: you can block just the Shorts and Reels inside an app and keep the rest of it. Friends' messages still come through, long YouTube videos still play. Or block the whole app, set daily limits, block websites, run schedules, or go strict. When you hit a wall you get a calm screen that says "Not now. You've got this." and one five minute pause per session.

The timers have no buttons. Stopwatch, Timer, Pomodoro and Clock open full screen. Swipe to switch, tap to start, long-press to reset, double-tap the digits to change the time. There are 20 clock faces and a set of 12 calm chimes.

Home is a glass orb that fills with liquid as you focus toward your daily goal, with your streak, sessions and a scroll budget under it. And there's chat with friends and groups that knows when you're focusing, so it stays quiet until you're done.

Everything here runs in my test build. New stuff lands often.

</details>
